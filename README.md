Evidence-grounded content operations for cross-border ecommerce: short-video and livestream scripts, UGC and creator briefs, multilingual captions, localized content angles, image/video briefs, content calendars, hook matrices and creative tests. It works across TikTok Shop, Instagram/Reels, Meta, Amazon, Shopify, Shopee, Lazada, AliExpress and Ozon.
